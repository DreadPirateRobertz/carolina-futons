The College Student's Furniture Starter Kit

What You Actually Need (And What You Don't)

✓
A futon. Sofa for hanging out + bed for crashing. The original dual-purpose furniture. One piece, two functions, zero wasted space.
✓
A decent mattress for it. Don't cheap out here. A 6" foam mattress works great for occasional sleeping & daily sitting. An 8" if it's a primary bed.
✓
A cover. Protects the mattress from dorm life. Machine washable. Comes in colors that hide evidence.
—
Skip the beanbag. You'll sit on the futon. Everyone sits on the futon. The beanbag becomes a laundry pile by October.

A Note for Parents

A quality futon is one of the few things you buy for college that your kid will actually keep using after graduation. A cheap one ends up on the curb at lease-end. Think of it as a 4-6 year investment, not a 9-month expense. The math works out — and you won't have to buy another one for their first apartment.
