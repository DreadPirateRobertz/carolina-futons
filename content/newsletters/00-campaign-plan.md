# Carolina Futons Newsletter Campaign Series

5. **education-02-murphy-beds.html** — Murphy cabinet beds: the room you didn't know you had
6. **education-03-mattress-science.html** — Inside an Otis Bed: what makes a mattress last 15 years
7. **education-04-wood-matters.html** — Why wood matters: rubberwood vs MDF vs metal

### Seasonal Promo (2 emails)
8. **seasonal-01-spring-refresh.html** — Spring room refresh: new starts in small spaces
9. **seasonal-02-back-to-school.html** — Back to school: dorm & first apartment essentials

### Re-engagement (2 emails)
10. **reengagement-01-miss-you.html** — "Your dream room is still waiting" (browse abandonment)
11. **reengagement-02-whats-new.html** — What's new at CF: new products + blog highlights

## Brand Guidelines
- Colors: Sand #F0F4F8, Espresso #1E3A5F, Mountain Blue #5B8FA8, Coral #4A7D94
- Font: Source Sans 3 (Google Fonts)
- Tone: Warm, quirky, expert but not stuffy, Hendersonville NC local flavor
- CTA style: Coral (#4A7D94) buttons, white text
- All emails mobile-responsive (single-column under 600px)
