Myth Busting

5 Things You Think You Know About Futons (That Aren't True)

Everyone's got a futon horror story from college. Thin mattress. Metal frame that pinched your fingers. Mysterious stains of unknown origin. Here's the thing: that was then.

Myth #1

"Futons are uncomfortable for sleeping"

Reality: A cheap futon is uncomfortable. A quality futon with an 8"+ Otis Bed innerspring mattress on a solid hardwood frame? That's a better sleeping surface than most $800 traditional mattresses. We have customers who've slept on theirs every night for 10+ years. The secret is the mattress — always invest in the mattress.

Myth #2

"Futons are for college kids and broke people"

Reality: Our best-selling frames are handcrafted hardwood furniture with better joinery than most "real" sofas. The Night & Day Seattle in Dark Chocolate looks like it belongs in Architectural Digest, not a dorm room. A quality futon is a design choice, not a budget compromise.

Myth #3

"They're hard to open and close"

Reality: This was true in 1998. Night & Day's MoonGlider mechanism converts with one hand. Literally. You pull the seat forward, the back drops flat. No wrestling, no cursing, no calling your roommate for help. Wall hugger frames are even simpler — they slide forward on their own.

Myth #4

"The mattress slides around and bunches up"

Reality: A properly sized mattress on a platform-base frame stays put. The issue happens when people put a thin mattress on a slatted frame with wide gaps. Our frames use either solid platforms or closely-spaced slats, and our mattresses are cut to fit the exact frame dimensions. No sliding, no bunching.

Myth #5

"They don't last"

Reality: Cheap futons don't last. Our rubberwood frames are rated for 500+ lbs and use traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery. KD Frames uses kiln-dried Virginia hardwood with a lifetime-of-the-product philosophy. We regularly hear from customers still using their frames after 15-20 years. The mattress will wear out long before the frame does.

The Real Test

Come to our Hendersonville showroom and sit on one. Lie on one. Fold it, unfold it. Feel the wood grain. Then tell us it's "just a futon." We dare you.
