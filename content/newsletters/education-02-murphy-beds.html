Murphy Cabinet Beds:
The Room You Didn't Know You Had

Imagine a Queen bed that, when you don't need it, transforms into an elegant chest the size of a dresser. No wall-mounting. No contractor. No rearranging your life.

How It Works

A Murphy cabinet bed is not the spring-loaded wall unit from sitcoms. It's a freestanding cabinet — about the size of a large dresser — that holds a folded Queen gel memory foam mattress inside. Open the top, pull out the mattress, unfold it. Done. Close it up, and it's a handsome piece of furniture with a usable surface on top.
