Welcome to the Family

We're thrilled you're here. Whether you're furnishing a first apartment, making your guest room work harder, or discovering that a futon is nothing like what you remember from 2003 — you've come to the right place.

We're a small showroom in Hendersonville, North Carolina, nestled in the Blue Ridge Mountains. We've been helping people find furniture that actually fits their lives — not the other way around.

What Makes Us Different

We don't sell furniture we wouldn't put in our own homes. Every frame, mattress, and accessory in our showroom has been chosen because it's genuinely well-made, sustainably sourced, and built to last years — not months.

Real Hardwood, Real Quality

Our Night & Day frames use plantation-grown Malaysian rubberwood — a sustainable hardwood that doesn't warp, crack, or squeak. KD Frames uses kiln-dried Virginia Tulip Poplar and manufactures right here in the Southeast.

Mattresses That Actually Last

Otis Bed mattresses are handcrafted in the USA with CertiPUR-US certified foam. A true 8" thick — not the compressed-to-sadness variety. Designed for 10-15 years of every-night sleeping.

Try Before You Buy

Come sit on every frame, lie on every mattress, and see every finish in person at our Hendersonville showroom. Some things you just need to feel. Our staff actually knows this stuff (ask us anything — we love geeking out about wood grain).

No Rainforest, No Guilt

Zero old-growth or rainforest wood in anything we sell. Period. Sustainability isn't a buzzword here — it's the baseline.
