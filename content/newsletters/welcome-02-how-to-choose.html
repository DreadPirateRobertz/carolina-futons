How to Choose Your Perfect Futon

It's actually pretty simple once you know the three things that matter. (Spoiler: the mattress matters more than the frame.)

1

Start with How You'll Use It

This is the question that determines everything. Are you sleeping on it every night? Using it as a guest bed a few times a year? Mainly sitting with occasional napping?

Every-night sleeper: Invest in the mattress. 8"+ innerspring or high-density foam. Solid hardwood frame with platform base.

Guest bed: A 6-8" foam mattress works great. You have more flexibility on the frame.

Mostly sitting: Focus on the frame's sofa ergonomics. Wall-hugger frames give you better back support.

2

Choose Your Frame

The frame determines your style, size options, and how smoothly it converts. Three things to check:

Wood type: Solid hardwood (rubberwood, poplar) > engineered wood > particleboard. Period.

Mechanism: Night & Day's MoonGlider is the smoothest. One-handed conversion, no wrestling required.

Wall hugger vs. traditional: Wall huggers slide forward as they recline — they sit flush against the wall in both positions. Traditional frames need 12-18" of clearance behind them.

3

Pick Your Mattress

This is where most people go wrong. They spend $800 on a frame and $100 on a mattress. Flip that thinking.

Minimum thickness for nightly use: 8 inches. Less and you'll feel the slats within months.

Core type: Innerspring = best support. High-density foam = best for folding. Hybrid = best of both.

Certification: Look for CertiPUR-US. It means the foam is tested for durability, content, and emissions. All our Otis Bed mattresses have it.
