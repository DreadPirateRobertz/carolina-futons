## Pin Production Specs

- **Dimensions:** 1000x1500px (2:3 ratio) — vertical format
- **File type:** JPG or PNG, high quality
- **Text on image:** Keep minimal. Title + 1 line. Brand colors (Sand bg, Espresso text, Mountain Blue accent, Coral for CTAs)
- **Logo:** Carolina Futons watermark, bottom corner, subtle
- **Rich Pins:** Enable for all product pages (auto-pulls price, availability, description)
- **Alt text:** Descriptive. Include "Carolina Futons" and product type for SEO.
