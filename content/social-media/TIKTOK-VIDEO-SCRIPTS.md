## Production Notes

- **Lighting:** Natural light preferred. Showroom shots during golden hour (late afternoon through west-facing windows).
- **Audio:** Trending sounds OK when they fit the content. Never forced. ASMR/ambient for product shots.
- **Text:** Always large, readable. White or Sand (#E8D5B7) text with Espresso (#3A2518) shadow. Mountain Blue (#5B8FA8) for emphasis.
- **Pacing:** First 3 seconds = hook or scroll. No slow intros. No "Hey guys."
- **Hashtags:** #CarolinaFutons #FutonLiving #SmallSpaceLiving #FurnitureTips + 3–5 trending tags per post
- **Posting:** Tue + Sat, between 11am–1pm EST or 7–9pm EST
