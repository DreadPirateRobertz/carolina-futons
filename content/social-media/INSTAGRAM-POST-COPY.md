### April 28 — Buying Checklist (Carousel)
Before you buy any futon — online or in-store — check these 3 things:

1. Frame material: Solid hardwood lasts 20+ years. Metal bends. Particle board splits.
2. Mattress thickness: 8 inches minimum for sleeping. Under 6? It's a pad, not a mattress.
3. Mechanism: Test the fold in person. Smooth action = quality hardware.

If you can't test it in person, you're guessing. Come feel the difference.
