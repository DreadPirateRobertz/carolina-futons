### April 2 — Mattress Rotation
Here's a tip from Brenda that costs nothing and adds 10 years to your mattress:

Rotate your futon mattress every 3 months. Top-to-bottom AND side-to-side. This evens out wear patterns and prevents body impressions from forming.

A quality futon mattress can last 5 years without rotation — or 15 years with it. That's a decade of extra comfort for 2 minutes of work, four times a year.

### April 9 — Spot Cleaning Tips
Brenda's been helping people take care of their furniture since 1991. Here's her go-to advice for spills:

1. Blot immediately — never rub. Rubbing pushes the stain deeper.
2. Use mild soap + cold water. Nothing harsh.
3. Let dry COMPLETELY before sitting or sleeping on it. Moisture trapped inside leads to mildew.
4. Better yet: invest in a washable cover. It's the smartest $60 you'll spend on your futon.

Prevention beats cleanup every time. Shop covers: carolinafutons.com
