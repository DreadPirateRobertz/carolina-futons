**About Carolina Futons**
Carolina Futons is a family-owned furniture retailer based in Hendersonville, NC, specializing in high-quality futon frames, mattresses, and covers. Known for solid hardwood construction, organic cotton options, and versatile convertible designs, Carolina Futons serves customers both in-store and online at carolinafutons.com. The company offers free local delivery in the Hendersonville/Asheville area and ships nationwide.

## Usage Notes

### Pitch Angles by Season

| Season | Angle | Outlets |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Jan-Feb | New Year home refresh, small-space resolutions | Apartment Therapy, The Spruce, local lifestyle |
| Mar-Apr | Spring cleaning / room makeover | Home decor blogs, Mountain Xpress |
| Jun-Aug | Back-to-school, dorm furnishing | College media, parent blogs, Sleepopolis |
| Sep-Oct | Fall home prep, guest room for holidays | Furniture Today, local papers, Wirecutter |
| Nov-Dec | Holiday gift guide, Black Friday | All consumer outlets, Hendersonville Times-News |

### Press Release Occasions

- New product line launch (frames, covers, mattresses)
- Store renovation or expansion
- Anniversary milestone
- Community event sponsorship (Apple Festival, Art on Main)
- Industry award or recognition
- Seasonal sale events
- Partnership announcements (local designers, campus housing)
- Sustainability initiative (organic materials, eco-friendly packaging)
